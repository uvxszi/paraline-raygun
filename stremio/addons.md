# Stremio addons for Raygun

Legal catalogues and tools that work in Raygun's Addons page (Library → Videos → Addons, or
Browse → Sources). Paste an address and press Add. Cinemeta and OpenSubtitles come with Raygun.

| Addon | Address | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinemeta | `https://v3-cinemeta.strem.io/manifest.json` | Film and series catalogues and details (Stremio's own) |
| OpenSubtitles v3 | `https://opensubtitles-v3.strem.io/manifest.json` | Subtitles |
| WatchHub | `https://watchhub.strem.io/manifest.json` | Where a title streams legally (links to the services) |
| Public Domain Movies | `https://caching.stremio.net/publicdomainmovies.now.sh/manifest.json` | Films in the public domain, playable in full |
| Stremio channels | `https://v3-channels.strem.io/manifest.json` | YouTube channels as catalogues |
| Pluto TV | `https://dev.nebulawp.org/stremio/pluto-tv-addon/manifest.json` | Pluto TV's free, ad-supported live channels |
| Radio | `https://1fe84bc728af-stremio-radios.baby-beamup.club/manifest.json` | Internet radio stations |

Addons are run by their authors: check one still answers before relying on it.
