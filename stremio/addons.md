# Stremio addons for Raygun

Legal addons that work in Raygun's Addons page (Library → Videos → Addons, or Browse → Sources). Paste an address and press Add.

| Addon | Address | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenSubtitles v3 | `https://opensubtitles-v3.strem.io/manifest.json` | Subtitles for films and series (also offered on the Addons page with one tap) |
| WatchHub | `https://watchhub.strem.io/manifest.json` | Where a title streams legally: links to the services that carry it |

WatchHub gives links for titles a catalogue addon lists. It works with Stremio's Cinemeta catalogue, added by its address: `https://v3-cinemeta.strem.io/manifest.json`.

Public-domain and openly licensed films are in Raygun's own sources (Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons, Blender open movies, NASA Image and Video Library), listed in Paraline's index.

## Not listed

| Addon | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Public Domain Movies | It answers, but its streams are BitTorrent links to 1080p copies, and its catalogue includes films whose public-domain status doesn't hold up (*Deep Red*, 1975, among them). |
| Archivio (Internet Archive films) | Its public instance is retired. |

Addons are run by their authors: check one still answers before relying on it.
